# Accounts & Denoms

## Denominations

| Field | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Native (base) denom | `{constants.nativeDenom}` | The **only** denom used for stateful accounting |
| Display denom | `{constants.displayDenom}` | Display metadata only |
| Symbol | `{constants.symbol}` | Display metadata only |
| Name | {constants.name} | Display metadata only |
| Decimals | {constants.decimals} | `1 {constants.symbol} = 10^{constants.decimals} {constants.nativeDenom}` |
| Max supply | {constants.maxSupply} | = {constants.maxSupplyDisplay} |

:::warning utwlt is the only accounting denom
`{constants.nativeDenom}` is the only denomination used by balances, minting,
rewards, and settlement. `{constants.displayDenom}` / `{constants.symbol}` /
"{constants.name}" are **display metadata only** and never appear in accounting
state. Rewards mint only `{constants.nativeDenom}`; releases pay only
`{constants.nativeDenom}`. CLI and query outputs are in `{constants.nativeDenom}`
unless explicitly formatted otherwise.
:::

## Premine

The production monetary design is expected to be **zero-premine**:
`CumulativeEmitted = 0`, and total supply rises only through epoch emission.

Development/localnet fixtures may explicitly fund accounts (e.g. the localnet
`init.sh` funds the authority and emergency accounts with
`1,000,000,000,000{constants.nativeDenom}` each). Any funded balance is a test
fixture, not a production premine — see [Localnet](localnet.md) and
[Status & Validation](status-and-validation.md).

## Module accounts

| Account | Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `{constants.rewardsModuleAccount}` | `Minter` | Holds minted epoch emission and unreleased entitlements; the only minter on the chain |
| `{constants.feePoolModuleAccount}` | _(none)_ | Dormant fee plumbing; holds nothing while fees are disabled |
| `fee_collector` | _(none)_ | The SDK's standard fee destination; the chain requires no fee, but any fee a sender attaches lands here and nothing pays out of it |
| `coreslot-authority` | _(none)_ | Keyless: the authority the SDK modules are bound to, so their own authority messages cannot be signed; also what `twilightd init` writes as the CoreSlot `authority` until a launch sets a real one |
| `coreslot-emergency` | _(none)_ | Keyless placeholder `twilightd init` writes as the CoreSlot `emergency_authority` |

All five are declared once in `app/config.go`, and that declaration also feeds the
economic-address rule: a module account is refused as a payout, settlement or treasury
address. Only `{constants.rewardsModuleAccount}` can mint; nothing can burn. Addresses
and queries are on [Module Accounts](../reference/module-accounts.md).

:::note Fees are dormant
Fee collection and fee distribution are **disabled** in the current version. The
`{constants.feePoolModuleAccount}` account exists as forward-compatible plumbing
only; its balance stays zero. Enabling fees requires a future coordinated upgrade.
:::
